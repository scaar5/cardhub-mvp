Applying the popular chromium finish to the stars of the Deutsche Fußball Liga (DFL), 2020-21 Topps Chrome Bundesliga Soccer gives collectors another chance to complete the set covering the German league.
Inside each Hobby box is one Chrome autograph along with several Refractors and inserts.
Another choice is found via the online-exclusive 2020-21 Topps Chrome Sapphire Bundesliga.
Also going the chromium route is 2020-21 Topps Chrome Match Attax Bundesliga.
See also: 2019-20 Topps Chrome Bundesliga Soccer Cards
## 2020-21 Topps Chrome Bundesliga Soccer Base
Leading off with the 100-card base set, 2020-21 Topps Chrome Bundesliga Soccer offers an original design instead of copying the flagship MLB look as in previous years.
There is also a larger parallel rainbow to chase this time, including multiple editions numbered to 250 or less.

Collectors can find short-printed cards for the Supercup championship trophy and the Derbystar match ball, as well.
## 2020-21 Topps Chrome Bundesliga Soccer Inserts
Adding to the non-hit ranks, Chrome Bundesliga features rising talent in Future Stars (1:6 packs).
Looking at those who were standouts during the last 10 Bundesliga seasons, the die-cut Decade of Dominance averages 1:9 packs.
Another choice is Top XI, which identifies the leading goal scorers from '19-20.
## 2020-21 Topps Chrome Bundesliga Soccer Autographs
Rounding out the 2020-21 Topps Chrome Bundesliga checklist are a few different autograph sets. The Base Chrome Autograph insert features Green Refractor (#/99), Gold Refractor (#/25), Orange Refractor (#/10), Red Refractor (#/5) and SuperFractor (1/1) parallels.
In addition, other signed inserts include Decade of Dominance Die-Cut Autographs, Future Stars Autographs and Top XI Autographs. Each of these cards is numbered to 99 copies.
Release Date: May 21, 2021
Product Configuration: 4 cards per pack, 18 packs per box, 12 boxes per case
