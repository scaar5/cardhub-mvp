2021-22 Topps Museum Collection UEFA Champions League "brings a touch of luxury and sophistication to UEFA Champions League trading cards yet again."
The premium UCL boxes contain one autograph, one autograph relic and one relic. All hits should be numbered.
See also: 2020-21 Topps Museum Collection UEFA Champions League
## 2021-22 Topps Museum Collection UEFA Champions League Base
Joining the three hits per box, 2021-22 Topps Museum Collection UEFA Champions League offers five cards from the base set.
The 75-card series highlights current stars along with legends and key rookies.
One card should be a limited parallel, numbered to 99 or less.
2021-22 Topps Museum Collection UEFA Champions League Base Parallels Breakdown
Amethyst - #/99
Sapphire - #/75
Gold - #/50
Ruby - #/25
Emerald - 1/1
## 2021-22 Topps Museum Collection UEFA Champions League Autographs
Among the hits, the 2021-22 Topps Museum Collection UEFA Champions League checklist covers three main groups.
Signed cards include returning options Archival Autographs and Archival Autographs Dual (#/25).
Another favorite, Framed Autographs comes in a metal frame.
The Rookie Reverence Autographs set is a debuting option for the latest batch of young talent.
## 2021-22 Topps Museum Collection UEFA Champions League Auto Relics
Memorabilia enters the mix with the other two hits in each box. 2021-22 Topps Museum Collection UEFA Champions League offers limited cards from Museum Autograph Relics, Momentous Material Auto Jumbo Relics, Rookie Reverence Dual Auto Relics, and Single Player Triple Relics Autos.
In addition, fans can chase one-of-one Framed Autograph Patch cards.
## 2021-22 Topps Museum Collection UEFA Champions League Relics
Relic cards act as the final box hit. Once again, all the possibilities are numbered.
Release Date: June 29, 2022
Product Configuration: 8 cards per pack, 1 pack per box, 12 boxes per case
