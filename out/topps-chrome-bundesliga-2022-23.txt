2022-23 Topps Chrome Bundesliga highlights key names from the top-flight German soccer league, including DFL stars, rookies and new signings such as Jamal Musiala, Jamie Bynoe-Gittens, Jude Bellingham, Paul Wanner, and Sadio Mané.
Collectors can count on one autograph in each Hobby box. Hobby case hits include one Wonders of Bundesliga insert and two Top XI inserts.
Although Topps provided Hobby Lite details with the initial solicitation, it was soon clarified that there is no Hobby Lite format this year.
See also: 2021-22 Topps Chrome Bundesliga Soccer Cards
## 2022-23 Topps Chrome Bundesliga Soccer Base / Refractors
The base set for 2022-23 Topps Chrome Bundesliga again features 100 cards.
The Refractor rainbow offers a mix of color and rarity with 299 copies or less for most editions. While the lineup is generally the same as last year, the Magenta Refractor (#/399) was not mentioned and several print run totals were changed.

## 2022-23 Topps Chrome Bundesliga Soccer Inserts
Next up, the 2022-23 Topps Chrome Bundesliga checklist turns to inserts. Airborne (1:15 packs) identifies nine of the best headers, Bright Future (1:6 packs) is home to 12 rising talents, and Flash of Genius (1:10 packs) covers 12 players with unique skill sets.
Acting as case hits, Top XI (1;120 packs; 2 per case) honors the league's best and Wonders of Time (1:240 packs; 1 per case) celebrates six all-time greats.
In addition, each insert adds limited sticker autograph versions, numbered to 99 or less, for select players.
## 2022-23 Topps Chrome Bundesliga Soccer Autographs
Speaking of signatures, 2022-23 Topps Chrome Bundesliga packs one sticker autograph card per Hobby box.
The primary Base Autographs line contains 95 players with multiple Refractor parallels limited to 165 copies or less.
Fans can also chase the debuting multi-signed cards in Dual Autographs (10 pairings; 1:1,898 packs) and Triple Autographs (5 trios; 1:11,375 packs).
Estimated Release Date: August 11, 2023
Hobby Configuration: 4 cards per pack, 18 packs per box, 12 boxes per case
