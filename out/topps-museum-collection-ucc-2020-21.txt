2020-21 Topps Museum Collection UEFA Champions League digs into another year of world-class club football.
The UCL Hobby boxes contain three hits that should break down as one autograph, one autograph relic and one relic.
See also: 2019-20 Topps Museum Collection UEFA Champions League
## 2020-21 Topps Museum Collection UEFA Champions League Base
The eight-card boxes of 2020-21 Topps Museum Collection UEFA Champions League include four base cards from the 75-card set. This includes top rookies.
Collectors can also find one limited parallel per box. Unlike other 2020-21 UEFA releases, the rainbow remains on the smaller side.

## 2020-21 Topps Museum Collection UEFA Champions League Autographs
Naturally, the 2020-21 Topps Museum Collection UEFA Champions League checklist provides multiple autographed inserts. These combine to fall one per box.
Returning staples include numbered Archival Autographs, Archival Dual Autographs and Framed Autographs.
A new option arrives via Lore of the League Autographs. The debuting inserts highlight seasoned superstars.

Look for Museum Autograph Relics, Museum Autograph Jumbo Relics, Single-Player Triple Relics Autographs and Museum Framed Autograph Patch cards.

## 2020-21 Topps Museum Collection UEFA Champions League Relics
Also found at a rate of one per box, the memorabilia cards in 2020-21 Topps Museum UCL feature a few well-known relics.
Choices include Meaningful Material Single Relics, Momentous Material Jumbo Relics, Momentous Material Prime Patch Relics and Single-Player Triple Relics.
There was also mention of all-new Triple-Player Team Crest Relics.
