2021-22 Topps Chrome Bundesliga Soccer supplies another chromium take on the German league.
In addition to Hobby boxes with one autograph, the new Hobby Lite format has exclusive parallels.
Another chromium format is 2021-22 Topps Chrome Sapphire Edition Bundesliga.
See also: 2020-21 Topps Chrome Bundesliga Soccer Cards
## 2021-22 Topps Chrome Bundesliga Soccer Base / Refractors
Highlighting 100 names, the base set for 2021-22 Topps Chrome Bundesliga sees an increased Refractor rainbow.
New choices include Magenta Refractor (#/399), Magenta Wave Refractor (#/399), Purple Wave Refractor (#/299), and Aqua Bubble Refractor (#/275).
Look for Gold RayWave Refractor parallels exclusively in Hobby Lite packs. These average 1:4 packs.

## 2021-22 Topps Chrome Bundesliga Soccer Inserts
2021-22 Topps Chrome Bundesliga Soccer turns to the inserts next. Golden Generation (1:9 packs) showcases 18 top players and Heroes of Tomorrow (1:6 packs) identifies 18 young stars.
Falling two per case, Top XI features the 11 active goal leaders from the '20-21 season.
## 2021-22 Topps Chrome Bundesliga Soccer Autographs
From there, the 2021-22 Topps Chrome Bundesliga checklist finishes with the signed cards.
Like the base set, there are more Refractor options in the Chrome Autographs series. Among the new editions are Blue Refractor (#/299), Purple Refractor (#/199), Prism Refractor (#/175), and Magenta Refractor (#/150) parallels.
Limited to 100 copies, B&W RayWave Refractor autographs are only found in Hobby Lite boxes. These average 1:20 boxes.
In addition, the inserts offer signed cards for select subjects, including Golden Generation (10 players; 100 copies or less) and Heroes of Tomorrow (10 players; 100 copies or less).
Top XI Autographs provides signatures for six players, also with a print run of 100 copies or less.
Release Date: July 27, 2022
Hobby Configuration: 4 cards per pack, 18 packs per box, 12 boxes per case
