Year three for the online-exclusive product, 2022-23 Topps Inception UEFA Club Competitions highlights Europe's best players and clubs with one autograph per box. New additions include relics, on-card autographs and top women's players.
Featured stars are pulled from the UEFA Champions League, UEFA Europa League, UEFA Europa Conference League, and UEFA Women's Champions League competitions.
The box price has increased to £125. For the 2020-21 and 2021-22 releases, the price was £100 on Topps UK.
UPDATE (May 30): Due to strong demand, the Topps site crashed and the sale was postponed one day. The boxes were sold through an EQL raffle on May 31.
Unfortunately we are going to have to cancel todays launch of Topps UCC Inception. Due to the incredibly high demand all of our websites crashed. We will be moving this to a @EQLofficial launch with details coming shortly. We are very sorry for the inconvenience.

## 2022-23 Topps Inception UEFA Club Competitions Set Details
2022-23 Topps Inception UEFA Club Competitions looks across Europe to fill out the set. Limited base parallels are also in play, falling two per box.
Among the hits, the 2022-23 Topps Inception UEFA Club Competitions checklist boasts a variety of signed cards, including Autographs, Dual Autographs, Dual Autograph Books, Autograph Relics, and Dual Auto Relic Books.
Collectors can also chase on-card Silver Signings autographs in the product for the first time. These include single and dual editions.
Unsigned Relics add another debut for card fans.
Release Date: May 30, 2022
Product Configuration: 7 cards per box
