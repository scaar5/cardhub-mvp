Back for a second season, 2021-22 Topps Stadium Club Chrome UEFA Champions League Soccer boasts "the most vivid photography of your favorite rookies, stars and legends."
The set stresses full-bleed card images along with limited inserts and Refractor parallels.
Hobby boxes guarantee one Chrome autograph.
See also: 2020-21 Topps Stadium Club Chrome UEFA Champions League
## 2021-22 Topps Stadium Club Chrome UCL Base / Refractors
2021-22 Topps Stadium Club Chrome UEFA Champions League Soccer again provides 100 cards in the base set.
However, the parallel rainbow sees an increase. New choices include Black & White (#/299), Night Vision Prism Refractor (#/175) and Sepia Prism Refractor (#/75), as well as the debuting dual-color Pink/Red Electric Charge Refractor, which is exclusive to Hobby boxes at a rate of one per box.

In addition, look for Base Image Variation cards and parallels for certain players.
## 2021-22 Topps Stadium Club Chrome UCL Inserts
Next up, the 2021-22 Topps Stadium Club Chrome UEFA Champions League Soccer checklist turns to the inserts.
Hobby boxes should contain several choices, including Championship Contributions (1:12 packs), for key UCL names, and Glimpses of Gold (1:14 packs), featuring some of the top young talent.
Reel Time (1:7 packs) revisits the established insert line for the brand.
The short-printed 1992 Beam Team (1:216 packs) also travels back to the past. These cards are exclusive to Hobby boxes.
## 2021-22 Topps Stadium Club Chrome UCL Autographs
Finally, collectors should expect one autograph per 2021-22 Topps Stadium Club Chrome UEFA Champions League Hobby box.
The possibilities include Chrome Autographs and Base Image Variations Autographs.
There are also Beam Team Autographs to chase.
Release Date: August 31, 2022
Hobby Configuration: 6 cards per pack, 18 packs per box, 12 boxes per case
