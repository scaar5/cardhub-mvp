Offering another chromium UCL set to chase, 2020-21 Topps Stadium Club Chrome UEFA Champions League is a brand debut for the European club tournament.
Boxes note that the new set is a Hobby-only product. Each Hobby box contains one autograph.
Of course, Chrome fans already have 2020-21 Topps Chrome UEFA Champions League, 2020-21 Topps Merlin Collection Chrome UEFA and 2020-21 Topps Chrome Match Attax UEFA.
## 2020-21 Topps Stadium Club Chrome UEFA Champions League Base / Refractors
Providing "some of the most unique and exhilarating imagery from throughout the season," 2020-21 Topps Stadium Club Chrome UEFA Champions League highlights the '20-21 stars on chromium stock.
The 100-card base set stresses "vivid photography" along with a rainbow of Refractor parallels. This includes the new Electric edition that showcases lightning-style color pairings.

In addition, select players can be found as base Image Variations. Low-numbered choices consist of Gold Refractor (#/50), Red Refractor (#/10), Black Refractor (#/5), and SuperFractor (1/1) editions.
## 2020-21 Topps Stadium Club Chrome UEFA Champions League Soccer Inserts
Appearing in every box, the 2020-21 Topps Stadium Club Chrome UEFA Champions League insert lineup features a shiny trio. Championship Contributions (1:12 packs) identifies the scoring threats and creators, Cup Holder's Honour (1:7 packs) covers the players who earned the right to rock the Badge of Honour patch, and Glimpses of Gold (1:14 Packs) is for the young UCL talent.
Among the insert parallels, look for Gold Refractor (#/50), Red Refractor (#/10), Black Refractor (#/5), and SuperFractor (1/1) cards.
## 2020-21 Topps Stadium Club Chrome UEFA Champions League Autographs
Combining to fall one per box, the 2020-21 Topps Stadium Club Chrome UEFA Champions League checklist finishes with autographs.
The base Chrome Autograph series supplies seven Refractors to hunt down.
Collectors can also try their luck with numbered Base Image Variation Autographs, Championship Contributions Autographs, Cup Holder's Honour Autographs, and Glimpses of Gold Autographs cards.
