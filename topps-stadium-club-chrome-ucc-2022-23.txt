Back for year three, 2022-23 Topps Stadium Club Chrome UEFA Club Competitions sees the product add UEFA Europa League players alongside UEFA Champions League players.
Hobby boxes again contain one autograph and several Refractor parallels.
See also: 2021-22 Topps Stadium Club Chrome UEFA Champions League
## 2022-23 Topps Stadium Club Chrome UEFA Base / Refractors
Stressing "jaw-dropping imagery," the 2022-23 Topps Stadium Club Chrome UEFA checklist includes 100 top names across Europe in the base set.
While many of the same Refractors are back, larger print runs appear for several.
In addition, new parallels include Pink Wave Refractor (#/150), Gold Prism Refractor (#/50) and Black/Gold Electric Charge Refractor (#/35).

Fans can also find short-printed photos for select names via the Members Only Rookie Image Variation series. However, these have a much smaller rainbow.
Each Hobby box should contain four base Refractors and four cards that can be color Refractors and/or image variations.
## 2022-23 Topps Stadium Club Chrome UEFA Inserts
2022-23 Topps Stadium Club Chrome UEFA Club Competitions is also home to several inserts.
The returning sets consist of Beam Team (1:24o packs), Championship Contributions (1:10 packs) and Glimpses of Gold (1:5 packs).
New options include Goal Force (1:5 packs) and Legends of Europe (1:20 packs).
Topps also teased "The Grail Awaits," which confirms the continuation of The Grail inserts set from previous '22-23 sets.
## 2022-23 Topps Stadium Club Chrome UEFA Autographs
Of course, 2022-23 Topps Stadium Club Chrome UEFA works in several autograph lines. The Chrome Autographs insert adds Green Refractor (#/99) parallels to the mix.
Playing up the photography angle, Behind the Lens Autographs is new, offering Photo Negative Refractor (#/25), Black & White Refractor (#/10) and Dark Room (#/5) editions.
Collectors can chase signatures in Beam Team Autographs, as well.
Showcasing a pair of autographs, the popular Co-Signers set makes its brand debut.
Lastly, the limited Members Only Rookie Image Variation Autographs (#'d) cards have only base versions and the one-of-one SuperFractor.
Release Date: August 28, 2023
Hobby Configuration: 6 cards per pack, 18 packs per box, 12 boxes per case
Giant/Mega Configuration: 5 cards per pack, 10 packs per box, 20 boxes per case
