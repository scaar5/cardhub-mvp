Delivering a premium card product, 2022-23 Topps Museum Collection UEFA Champions League keeps the one-pack-per-box format.
Every Hobby box contains one autograph, one relic and one autograph relic.
The framed autograph cards act as a case hit.
Box preorders from Topps appeared on June 19 for $274.99.

## 2022-23 Topps Museum Collection UEFA Champions League Base
Starting with the base set, 2022-23 Topps Museum Collection UEFA Champions League highlights 75 top names from the annual club competition.
Each box has four base cards and one base parallel.
Look for six parallels in the rainbow, with each numbered to 125 or less.
2022-23 Topps Museum Champions League Base Parallels Breakdown
Copper - #/125
Amethyst - #/99
Sapphire - #/75
Gold - #/50
Ruby - #/25
Emerald - 1/1
## 2022-23 Topps Museum Collection UEFA Champions League Autographs
The rest of the 2022-23 Topps Museum Collection UEFA Champions League checklist is dedicated to hits.
The signature lineup includes Archival Autographs (#'d), Archival Dual Autographs (#/25) and Rookie Reverence Autographs (#'d).
Collectors can also chase the case-hit Framed Autographs cards, numbered to 99 copies (or less).
Another choice is the Legendary Ink Framed Autographs (#/25) insert. These boast on-card autographs.
Limited to five copies, the Supreme Script Autograph Book cards feature 10 autographs on each six-paneled card.
## 2022-23 Topps Museum Collection UEFA Champions League Auto Relics
Auto-relic fans have serial-numbered Museum Autograph Relics, Momentous Material Autograph Jumbo Relics and Single Player Signature Swatches Dual Relic Autographs cards.
Some of the rarest cards from 2022-23 Topps Museum Collection UEFA Champions League appear in Supreme Script Autograph Relic Book (#/5) and Museum Framed Autograph Patch (1/1).
## 2022-23 Topps Museum Collection UEFA Champions League Relics
The memorabilia cards supply the third box hit for 2022-23 Topps Museum Collection UEFA Champions League.
The selection consists of Meaningful Material Single Relics (#'d), Momentous Material Jumbo Relics (#'d), Momentous Material Prime Patch Relics (#/5), Rookie Reverence Relics (#'d), Single-Player Triple Relics (#'d), and Supreme Materials Relic Books (#/25).
Release Date: July 24, 2023
Product Configuration: 8 cards per pack, 1 pack per box, 12 boxes per case
