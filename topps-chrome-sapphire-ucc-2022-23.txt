2022-23 Topps Chrome Sapphire Edition UEFA Club Competitions marks the fourth year of the online-exclusive product.
Formerly a UEFA Champions League set, Sapphire Edition now includes Champions League, Europa League and Europa Conference League players.
Instead of a standard online sale, the exclusive Topps.com release arrives via EQL raffle on August 7, 2023, at 11 AM (Eastern). Collectors have two hours to enter the drawing for a chase to purchase a box. This is the first instance of an EQL raffle for the Topps US site, but Topps UK releases have used it in the past.
Box prices have dropped slightly, coming in at $225. The boxes were $249.99 last year.
See also: 2021-22 Topps Chrome Sapphire UEFA Champions League
## 2022-23 Topps Chrome Sapphire Edition UEFA Club Competitions Set Details
Covering top names across Europe, the 2022-23 Topps Chrome Sapphire Edition UEFA Club Competitions checklist has the same 200-card base set as 2022-23 Topps Chrome UEFA Club Competitions.
Of course, the key difference is the signature Sapphire Edition style, which adds a blue color scheme to a distinct cracked ice Refractor finish. Each box has two Sapphire parallels that are numbered to 99 or less.
Another possibility comes with the Image Variation (1:8 packs) cards that should fall in every box. These match the base parallel rainbow, but come with much tougher odds.

Purple Sapphire - #/15 (1:1,382 packs)
Red Sapphire - #/5 (1:4,089 packs)
Padparadscha - 1/1 (1:20,036 packs)
2022-23 Topps Chrome Sapphire Edition UEFA Club Competitions supplies inserts, as well. Falling in every fifth box, Sapphire Selections (1:40 packs) is a new choice for collectors.
On the other hand, Wonderkids (1:4 packs) is much easier to pull, averaging two per box.
For the first time, fans even have a shot at signed cards thanks to Sapphire Selections Autographs (#'d; 1:687 packs). These rare autographs also come as serial-numbered Gold Refractor (1:682 packs), Red Refractor (1:4,453 packs) and SuperFractor (1:20,037 packs) versions.
Release Date: August 7, 2023 (via EQL raffle)
Product Configuration: 4 cards per pack, 8 cards per box
