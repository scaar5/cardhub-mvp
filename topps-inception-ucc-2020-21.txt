Likely familiar to MLB and NFL collectors, 2020-21 Topps Inception UEFA Champions League marks the brand's standalone debut in European soccer.
The Topps.com exclusive first appeared on Topps UK for £100 per box and Topps Germany for €115 (around $135-$140 at the time).
It sold out quickly and never made it to the Topps US site.
## 2020-21 Topps Inception UEFA Champions League Set Details
With multiple subsets, the 2020-21 Topps Inception UEFA Champions League checklist highlights past and present stars. Themed groupings in the 70-card base set include Cloud 9, Emerging Stars, Legends, Magnificent 7, Perfect 10, Star Quality, and Succession.
Each box adds a numbered base parallel that is limited to 99 copies or less.
In addition, the signed cards apply an autograph for select names in the base set. There are also short prints.
UEFA legends fill out the series with Dual Autograph and Patch Autograph inserts.
Every seven-card box should contain one autograph.
Shop for available UEFA boxes at Topps.
Release Date: August 9, 2021
Product Configuration: 7 cards per pack, 1 pack per box
