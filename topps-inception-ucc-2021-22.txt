Returning for year two, 2021-22 Topps Inception UEFA Club Competitions again arrives as an online-exclusive set.
The colorful Topps UK product features one autograph per box for £100. The box price was also £100 last year.
Boxes are available at other Topps European stores, as well.
One key difference from last year is that it is no longer based solely on the UEFA Champions League. This allows for Champions League cards alongside Europa League and Europa Conference League cards.
See also: 2020-21 Topps Inception UEFA Champions League Cards
## 2021-22 Topps Inception UEFA Club Competitions Set Details
Nevertheless, 2021-22 Topps Inception UEFA Club Competitions largely mirrors the debut edition for 2020-21. Look for multiple subsets in the base lineup along with colorful parallels.
The autograph cards combine to fall one per box and include base autographs and autograph relics.
Featuring a dual-panel layout, the dual and quad booklet autograph cards are a debut option.
Release Date: July 29, 2022
Product Configuration: 7 cards per box
